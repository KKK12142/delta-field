Art style B, handmade plush toys: every animal is a stuffed plush toy made of soft felt and short fleece fabric with visible fabric texture, neat stitched seams, embroidered mouths and noses, shiny round button eyes, slightly squashy shapes, warm cozy colors.
Top row, front view standing, eight different animals side by side: an orange tabby cat, a light brown puppy with floppy ears, a white bunny with long ears, a brown bear cub, a panda, a red fox, a penguin chick, a golden hamster.
Middle row, the same characters in lively poses: the cat walking mid-step, the bunny waving one paw, the fox jumping with both arms up, the bear cheering, the penguin doing a happy little hop.
Bottom row, showing how students can tell twins apart: the same cat design in three different fur colors (gray, black and white, cream), then the puppy wearing a small baseball cap, then the panda wearing a cozy scarf.
