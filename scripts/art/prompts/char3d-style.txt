Original character concept sheet for a cute, simple 3D multiplayer game used in a high school science class.
Render style: soft low-poly 3D toy render, smooth matte plastic or vinyl surfaces, simple rounded shapes, gentle studio lighting, soft contact shadow under each figure.
Each character is one simple body shape in one solid body color, easy to rebuild as a low-poly 3D model with few parts. Arms and legs are very short or absent; the body itself carries the cuteness.
Cute face with two big simple eyes painted on the front of the body (no visor, no helmet, no glass window). Faces are friendly and readable from far away.
Layout: one clean sheet on one flat solid light gray background, characters evenly spaced in neat rows, every character fully visible and not overlapping.
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no signature, no frames.
This is an original design. Do not imitate any existing game, franchise, mascot, brand, studio style, or real person.
