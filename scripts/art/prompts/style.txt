Original pixel art for a cheerful top-down 2D school game used in a high school science class.
Strict pixel art: every game pixel is a crisp, solid square on one uniform grid, drawn large so the grid is easy to see.
No anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no glow, no soft shadows, no noise, no dithering.
Small limited palette (about 16 colors), flat colors with 2-3 shades per material, light coming from the top-left.
1-pixel outline in a dark warm navy (not pure black) around characters and objects.
Background: one flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) color with no texture, no shadow, and no floor (it is removed later).
No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no grid lines, no borders, no watermark, no signature.
Do not imitate any existing game, franchise, studio style, or real person.
